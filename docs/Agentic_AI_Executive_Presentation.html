02 · The opportunity

Why AI agents belong in a VOC

An agent is software that pursues a goal over your data — it reads, cross-references, reasons and drafts conclusions on its own. Not scripted rules. Not a generic chatbot.

🔍

Tireless coverage

Reads every finding and every asset relationship, on every run. Nothing is skipped because it's Friday at 5 p.m.

🧭

Context, not just severity

Ranks by what a vulnerability lets an attacker do here — reachability, credentials, ownership, business criticality.

⚖️

Consistent rigor

The same depth of analysis on run 1 and run 100. Expertise becomes repeatable instead of person-dependent.

📎

Evidence-bound

Every claim is anchored to a record in our own data. A statement that can't be sourced is never made.
